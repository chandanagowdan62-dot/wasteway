Problem Statement
From confusion at home to informed waste decisions
The Everyday Confusion
A household waste item may look simple, but its material, condition and contamination can change how it should be handled.
The Hidden Consequence
Incorrect segregation can mix recoverable materials with unsuitable waste and make safe processing more difficult.
The AI Opportunity
An image-based intelligent assistant can help users recognise waste and understand the next suitable action.
“The problem is not only where waste goes — it is that people often do not know what the waste actually needs.”
Our project aims to convert a simple waste image into a clear, practical and responsible action.

Abstract
An AI-assisted approach to household waste understanding
Project Concept
The proposed system uses Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning to classify household waste from an input image. After prediction, the system provides meaningful guidance related to reuse, recycling, collection or special handling.
Proposed Intelligence Layer
The system is designed to move beyond a category label by connecting classification with an action recommendation and a digital record that can support responsible waste handling.

Objectives
Designed around recognition, understanding and responsible action
01  Intelligent Waste Recognition
Develop an image classification model that identifies selected household waste categories from images.
02  Context-Aware Understanding
Use the predicted category to explain the waste type and highlight relevant handling considerations.
03  Smart Action Recommendation
Suggest a suitable next step such as reuse, repair, recycling, collection or special handling.
04  Digital Waste Passport
Create a simple digital record containing the predicted category, recommendation and user-confirmed result.
